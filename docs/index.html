About

About me

I'm a software developer with more than seven years of experience in Python and more than six years in Django. My expertise extends to Angular, where I have worked for more than three years. I'm an autodidact, always seeking to expand my knowledge and skills.

At Chekin I had the opportunity to lead a small team of two developers, where we migrated and refactored a deprecated part of the code of a platform that automates check-in for providers such as Booking.com and Airbnb.

I have worked for companies in Argentina, the USA, and Spain, including Moni, a Fintech that provides more than 60,000 loans per year; Examity, an anti-cheat system for test-takers used by more than 60 universities in the USA; and Venmo, the well-known wallet by PayPal.

In my free time I (really) love to play football with friends, spend time with my family, and build new things just for fun.
